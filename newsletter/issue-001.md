## Who requires what: quick reference

| Owner | Max age (oil/chem tanker) | SIRE age | CAP trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| bp | 20 yrs (>5,000 dwt) | < 6 months | CAP 2, >20k dwt, from 15 yrs* |
| ADNOC PPA | 20 yrs (>5,000 DWT) | ≤ 6 months + ADNOC SIRE ≤ 24 months | CAP 2 hull, >15 yrs & >20k DWT |
| RightShip baseline | 25 yrs | SIRE/CDI ≤ 6 months | CAP 2 H/M/C, >20k DWT from 15 yrs |
| Repsol | 25 yrs | OCIMF ≤ 6 months (more if ≥ 20 yrs) | CAP 2 H/M/C, ≥15 yrs & >5k SDWT |
| Trans Mountain | <15, up to 20 with CAP 2 | SIRE/OVID ≤ 7 months | CAP 2 Hull & MC, 15–20 yrs |
| Port of Fujairah | >25 yrs not permitted | — | — |

\*bp's own pages disagree (15 yrs / 3rd special survey vs 20 yrs / 4th special survey). [Full table with sources →](../table.html)
