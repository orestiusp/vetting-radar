RightShip tightens tanker PSC, age, CAP and paperwork rules (Rev 4.0)

Max age 25 yrs tanker / 25 LPG / 35 LNG3+ PSC detentions in 24 mo = UnacceptableCAP 2 from 15 yrs (>20k DWT)CAP valid 36 monthsSIRE/CDI ≤ 6 monthsCrew matrix ≤ 30 daysHVPQ ≤ 3 months
  • Rewritten baseline, dated 1 Oct 2026
  • New rule: unknown vessel attributes (e.g. class, flag, P&I, ownership) = Unacceptable until verified
  • PSC detention, deficiency and close-out rules consolidated
  • Incident review periods split: Category A/B vs C/D

What to do: Check each vessel's RightShip profile for unknown attributes and recent PSC detentions.

Details

Rev 4.0 (dated 1 Oct 2026) rewrites the Oil, Gas & Chemical baseline. Revisions log: expanded vetting policy and Safety Score treatment; new 'unknown vessel attributes' rule; consolidated PSC rules (3+ detentions in 24 months = Unacceptable); Category A/B vs C/D incident review periods; refined age/CAP/SIRE-CDI/crew matrix/HVPQ rules (max age 25 tanker / 25 LPG / 35 LNG; CAP 2 with Level 2 FDA, 36-month validity; crew matrix ≤30 days; HVPQ ≤3 months).

CDI adds 10 alternative-fuel chapters to the SIR and renumbers questions

New chapters 15–24Launch Mon 14 Dec 202610th Edition SIR
  • New AEPS questionnaire: biofuels, methanol/ethanol, ammonia, LPG/LNG as fuel
  • Also WAPS, onshore power, fuel cells, ShaPoLi/EPL, onboard carbon capture
  • Section and question numbering will change
  • Applies to all vessel and inspection types CDI covers

What to do: Remap CDI question numbers in your pre-vetting checklists before 14 Dec 2026.

Details

CDI's 'Alternative Energy & Power Supply' questionnaire adds chapters 15–24 to the 10th Edition Ship Inspection Report (biofuels, methanol/ethanol, ammonia, LPG and LNG as fuel, WAPS, onshore power, fuel cells, ShaPoLi/EPL, onboard carbon capture), with section/question renumbering. Official launch scheduled Monday 14 December 2026.

OCIMF updates its guidance on safety-critical equipment and spares

Replaces 2018 edition
  • Risk-based approach to identifying critical equipment and spares
  • Covers documenting and maintaining them within an ISM-aligned SMS

What to do: Compare your SMS critical-equipment and spares procedures with the revised paper.

Details

OCIMF published the revised paper (replaces the 2018 first edition): a risk-based approach to identifying, documenting and maintaining safety-critical equipment and spares within an ISM-aligned SMS. Relevant to SIRE 2.0 defect/PMS questions.
