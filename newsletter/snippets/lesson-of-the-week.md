## Lesson of the week

**[Spring line parted under sudden load during a lock entry: one fatality](https://vettingradar.com/lessons/mooring-spring-parted-lock-entry-2018.html?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=lesson-of-the-week)**

A chemical tanker was entering a sea lock when a forward spring that had just been paid out came under heavy load. The mooring team could not react in time, the line parted and the part still on the winch whipped back and killed a crew member. The investigators found four safety barriers that failed on the day.

**Check on board:**

- Mooring plan for the next port or lock briefed to all stations, including when lines go under load.
- Snap-back zones assessed for the actual leads in use, and safe positions marked.
- Enough crew at each station so the officer in charge can supervise.
