## Lesson of the week

**[Torch used beside an open tank opening: unpermitted hot work caused a fatal explosion](https://vettingradar.com/lessons/hot-work-torch-beside-open-tank-explosion-2025.html?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=lesson-of-the-week)**

During loading alongside, the chief engineer of a liquid bulk tank vessel used a small propane torch on deck to free broken bolts on a cargo tank hatch cover. Fittings had been taken off the cover, leaving open holes into a tank whose cargo gave off flammable gas. The flame ignited the gas and the tank exploded, killing the chief engineer and injuring three crew.

**Check on board:**

- Hot work procedure and permit on board, in line with ISGOTT and the SMS, and crew can say when it applies (including small torches and heat guns).
- Recent hot work permits complete: location, gas readings, isolation of nearby tanks and lines, fire watch and the required sign-offs.
- Portable torches, grinders and heat guns kept in a controlled place and issued only against a permit.
