Dry Bulk Vessel Vetting Baseline Criteria
Purpose
This document outlines the RightShip recommended minimum Baseline Criteria for
vetting services specific to dry bulk (and general cargo) vessels. These criteria can
be customised based on the specific needs and risk profiles of each vetting
customer. The tailored requirements are then used to establish final vetting rules,
which guide the vetting process and inform vessel recommendations, considering
factors such as safety, environmental impact, and crew wellbeing.
RightShip Vetting Policy
 There is no RightShip minimum Safety Score. Any vessel may be vetted with a Safety
Score between 1/5 and 5/5 and could be positively recommended by RightShip
provided that the agreed vetting customer requirements are met.
 Vessels with a lower Safety Score may require additional vessel vetting checks, which
may include completion of a RightShip Inspection before we can consider a positive
recommendation to our vetting customers.
 Vessels with a 0/5 or 1/5 Safety Score are unlikely to be positively recommended until
the reason for the score has been investigated and resolved to the satisfaction of
RightShip.
 Vessels with a N/A Safety Score may not be covered by our normal vessel vetting
services and may require the provision of additional information and additional checks
if a customer requires RightShip to vet a vessel with a N/A Safety Score.

Dry Bulk Vessel Vetting Baseline Criteria
Checks performed within the vetting process
Checks performed during the vetting process include but are not limited to the
following:
 Full review of the Safety Score including assessment of the veracity of data
contributing to its calculated value.
 Verification, with input from the vessel’s DOC Company, of data for missing defects,
adverse reports, incidents not yet captured by the Safety Score.
 Assessment of Port State Control Inspection performance beyond that illustrated by
the Safety Score, including status and severity of deficiencies.
 Review of any missing Port State Control data, for example from Riyadh and Abuja
MoU’s is captured and uploaded to allow for recalculation of the Safety Score.
 Assessment of incident performance beyond that illustrated by the Safety Score,
including the status and severity of incidents.
 Assessment of vessel performance based on completed RightShip Inspections
beyond that illustrated by the Safety Score, including the status, severity, recurrence
and closure of findings.
 Assessment of the vessel’s DOC Company / Technical Managers performance beyond
that illustrated by the Safety Score and/or DOC Subscore.
 Verification of the vessel’s current Class status, including the status and validity of key
Certificates, Surveys and the presence of any overdue Conditions of Class which might
have operational impacts or reveal an unreported recent incident.
 Open Abandonment of Seafarers case(s) involving the subject vessel, or its associated
owning or managing entities.
 Confirmation that requirements for a RightShip Inspection due to vessel age and/or
performance, have been maintained.
 Review against Vessel or Company Restrictions set by RightShip and/or the vetting
customer.
 Update and assessment of details of recent vessel changes of management or new
build deliveries.
 Checks of current trading status of the vessel, including (but not limited to) In
Casualty/Repairing, Laid Up.
 Assessment of previous vetting history.

Dry Bulk Vessel Vetting Baseline Criteria
Conditions that always lead to a vessel being recommended as Unacceptable
Binary outcome based on RightShip Baseline Criteria:
 Vessel age exceeds the RightShip standard.
 Open Abandonment of Seafarers cases involving the subject vessel.
 Vessel banned by a Port State Control Authority.
 Vessel or company restricted by RightShip due to ongoing concerns.
 An “Unacceptable” last RightShip Inspection.
 Vessel which exceeds the RightShip Inspection age trigger and does not have a valid
RightShip Inspection, or evidence that a RightShip Inspection has been completed or is
scheduled.
 Vessel flagged with a Paris or Tokyo MoU scored flag, which is Very High Risk, if
vessel doesn’t hold a valid RightShip Inspection.
 Vessel flagged with a Paris or Tokyo MoU scored flag, which is on their Blacklist, and
combined with a Paris or Tokyo MoU Low or Very Low performing Class Society.
Subject to clarification through the Vetting Process:
 Vessel which has been De-Classed or Class Suspended as a result of performance
and failure to comply with Class requirements.
 Vessel that is falsely presented.
 Vessel Operator demonstrating an unwillingness to respond to RightShip during the
vetting process.
 Vessel with overdue Class and Statutory Surveys, overdue Conditions of Class and/or
overdue significant memos.
 Vessel Operator demonstrating unwillingness to undergo a RightShip Inspection due
to vessel age and/or performance.
 Vessel’s Class, Flag, P&I provider, or DOC Company details.

Dry Bulk Vessel Vetting Baseline Criteria
Conditions that may lead to a vessel being recommended as Unacceptable
In most cases the recommendation of unacceptable will be a result of an
accumulation of the risk factors shown below as opposed to one of the items being
observed in isolation.
 Recurrent issues highlighting failures in Safety Management System.
 Lack of acceptable response to enable satisfactory close out of an incident/adverse
report.
 Cases with the same ship manager where serious incidents have occurred and have
not been disclosed.
 Open Abandonment of Seafarers case(s) involving the subject vessel or operators still
under review after a closed Abandonment of Seafarer case(s).
 Vessels or Companies under review by RightShip due to ongoing concerns.
 Vessels flagged with a Targeted, Medium to High or High Risk Flag as designated by
Tokyo or Paris MoU, if vessel does not also have a valid RightShip Inspection.
 Excessive number of incidents of any category in a particular time frame.
 Multiple PSC Detentions over a timeframe – typically 3 such events over 24 months.
 Excessively high ratios of Deficiencies/PSC Inspection over 24 months, with port
averaging taken into consideration.
 Consistently unacceptable close-outs.
 Crew Experience and/or length of service falling below industry standards and DOC
Company’s response suggests a crew change is not imminent.
 Non-International Association of Classification Societies (IACS) which have been rated
as High Risk in published Lists by recognised authorities or bodies.
 Vessel or Company Restrictions set by the vetting customer.
 Vessel under the management of a DOC Company which is on any MOU refusal of
access or letters of warning list.
 Vessel holding a RightShip Inspection that was awarded only 3 months validity.

Dry Bulk Vessel Vetting Baseline Criteria
What would trigger additional Marine Subject Matter Expert Review?
 Port State Control Inspections with deficiencies with an open status1 within the
RightShip Platform within a given time frame (typically the last 12 months).
 Port State Control Inspections with deficiencies presenting elevated risk factors
regardless of status and/or time frame.
 Incidents with an open status1 within the RightShip Platform within a given time frame
(typically the last 24 months for Category A & B and 12 months for Category C & D).
 Incidents presenting elevated risk factors regardless of status and/or time frame.
 Class Survey Status Report not updated, expired or expiring Certificates/Surveys,
investigation into the reason for issuance of Short-Term Conditional Certification,
Conditions of Class for operational impacts.
 Vessels exhibiting unknown details in key areas - Class, Flag, P&I, Ownership and
Management details.
 Recent change of management or new build delivery will normally necessitate
completion of a RightShip MOC/VTOP (Management of Change / Vessel Take Over
Process) Questionnaire for review during the vessel vetting process.
 Unscheduled Dry Docking or Repair periods require investigation to ascertain the
reasons for such.
1
Open Status = RightShip has not yet received, assessed and closed the vessel
operator’s PSC Inspection Closeout Submission or Incident Investigation Report
including Root Cause Analysis, Corrective Action(s) and Preventative Measure(s)
Plan.
