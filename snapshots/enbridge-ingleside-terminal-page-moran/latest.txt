Additional Information
Restrictions and Limitations
Bunkers by barge are allowed Lube oils by truck are not allowed Stores are allowed via barge or across the dock Crew change is allowed Terminal does charge for deliveries
Max allowed draft is 52 ft SW
Pls note additional water depth is available in most areas of the docks. Soundings are available upon request
Security Requirements
Visitors must have TWIC and be on the agent's gatelist. Terminal must be advised of deliveries 24 hours in advance or by Friday if the vessel is calling on the weeken
