How does the “Send a mail” in the third step work?
It is a “mailto” hyperlink, which opens a new message in your system default email client. If you don’t have any installed (or in case it doesn´t work) please send an email with the required attachments and information to the email addresses indicated in step 3.
What information/attachments are to be included in your email in case the “Send a mail” hyperlink doesn´t work ?
