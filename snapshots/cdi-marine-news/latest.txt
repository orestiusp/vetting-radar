CDI Announces “Alternative Energy & Power Supply” (AEPS) Questionnaire
At the request of CDI Marine Chemical member companies, CDI established a dedicated working group to develop and produce its new “Alternative Energy & Power Supply” (AEPS) Questionnaire.
Objectives
The following are the key objectives:
1. To incorporate alternative marine fuels and power sources into the ongoing development of the CDI Marine Ship Inspection Report (SIR).
2. To support CDI stakeholders, participants, and vessel operators in managing the risks associated with emerging fuels and power technologies through CDI's marine inspection programme.
3. To contribute to the International Maritime Organization's (IMO) objective of achieving net-zero greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions within the shipping industry.
4. To identify ways in which CDI can support the IMO transition and assist participants and vessel operators through its inspection framework.
5. To develop rapid-response inspection protocols covering alternative fuels and power sources as they emerge.
6. To evaluate options for incorporating these protocols into the existing CDI 10th Edition Ship Inspection Report (SIR).
Development of the AEPS Questionnaire
Working in close collaboration with CDI stakeholders, CDI has developed a comprehensive AEPS inspection protocol and supporting Guidance Notes (GN) covering a range of alternative fuels and energy sources.
The AEPS questionnaire is designed to ensure the continued provision of high-quality marine assurance information, addressing the risks associated with these technologies in the following key areas:
• Personal safety
• Personal protective equipment and practices
• Operational safety
• Crew competency and training
• Environmental protection, health, and safety
• Certification and compliance
• Firefighting and emergency response
New AEPS Chapters in the 10th Edition Ship Inspection Report (SIR)
The AEPS questionnaire introduces the following new chapters to the existing 10th Edition Ship Inspection Report (SIR)
• Chapter 15 – Marine Biofuels
• Chapter 16 – Methanol and Ethanol as Fuel
• Chapter 17 – Ammonia as Fuel
• Chapter 18 – LPG as Fuel
• Chapter 19 – LNG as Fuel
• Chapter 20 – Wind Assisted Propulsion Systems (WAPS)
• Chapter 21 – Onshore Power Supply (OPS)
• Chapter 22 – Fuel Cells
• Chapter 23 – Shaft Power Limitation (ShaPoLi) and Engine Power Limitation (EPL) Systems
• Chapter 24 – Onboard Carbon Capture (OCC)
Integration with the CDI Ship Inspection Report
The new AEPS chapters (15-24) will become an integral part of the existing 10th Edition Ship Inspection Report (SIR), with associated updates to section and question numbering.
The AEPS content will be incorporated into:
• The full CDI Ship Inspection Report (SIR)
• The CDI Marine Report Editor Inspector software
• The CDI Marine database
These changes will apply to all vessel types and Marine inspection types currently covered by CDI.
Availability and Launch
CDI will shortly release PDF versions of the AEPS questionnaire, next week, which will be available for download from the CDI website.
The official launch of CDI’s “ Alternative Energy & Power Supply” (AEPS) Questionnaire is scheduled for Monday 14th December 2026

CDI - LCO2 Inspection Announcement
Following the launch of its innovative Liquid Carbon Dioxide (LCO2) Vessel, Inspection Program; a pioneering initiative aimed at ensuring the safe and sustainable transport of LCO2 onboard specialised ships designed to transport reclaimed liquefied carbon dioxide for carbon capture and storage (CCS) projects.
CDI is pleased to announce the completion of its first LCO2 inspection under the CDI-Marine Inspection scheme.
As global efforts intensify to combat climate change, the industry is increasingly turning to carbon capture and storage (CCS) technologies to reduce emissions from sectors that are slower to decarbonise. The safe and efficient transport of Liquefied CO2 by maritime vessels is a critical component of this strategy.
CDI’s LCO2 inspection program sets a benchmark for vessel integrity, operational safety, and environmental stewardship in this emerging sector.
Key Features of the CDI LCO2 Ship Inspection Program:
• Specialised LCO2 Inspection Protocols tailored to the unique properties and handling requirements of LCO2.
• Enhanced Safety Standards aligned with international regulations and industry practices.
• Environmental Risk Assessments to ensure minimal ecological impact during transport.
• Digital Reporting Tools for streamlined data collection and transparency.
The LCO2 Ship Inspection Program is available to CDI members and stakeholders involved in the operation and chartering of specialised vessels transporting Liquefied Carbon Dioxide.

• Bahrain
• UAE
• Qatar

• Southern Saudi Arabia
• Kuwait
• Israel
• Egypt : (Sinai, Eastern part of Ismailiyah Governorate, Western Desert)
• Iran
• Iraq
• Syria
• Yemen
• Jordan : (Jordan/Syrian Border)
1. CDI inspections that are requested in other CDI Middle East Zone regions, will be considered only on a case by case basis
2. CDI “WILL NOT” be conducting any CDI inspections in the regions listed above due to safety reasons
3. CDI will continue to monitor and review this situation and update as required

CDI Inspection Reports Compliance

CDI Launching “Liquid Carbon Dioxide (LCO2) Vessel” - Ship Inspection Program
CDI is Launching Ground-breaking “Liquid Carbon Dioxide (LCO2) Vessel” - Ship Inspection Program
The Stichting Chemical Distribution Institute (CDI) is proud to announce the launch of its innovative Liquid Carbon Dioxide (LCO2) Vessel, Inspection Program; a pioneering initiative aimed at ensuring the safe and sustainable transport of LCO2 onboard specialised ships designed to transport reclaimed liquefied carbon dioxide for carbon capture and storage (CCS) projects.
- This new program will go live on the 12th November 2025
As global efforts intensify to combat climate change, the industry is increasingly turning to carbon capture and storage (CCS) technologies to reduce emissions from sectors that are slower to decarbonise.
The safe and efficient transport of Liquefied CO2 by maritime vessels is a critical component of this strategy.
CDI’s new inspection program sets a benchmark for vessel integrity, operational safety, and environmental stewardship in this emerging sector.
Key Features of the LCO2 Ship Inspection Program:
• Specialised Inspection Protocols tailored to the unique properties and handling requirements of LCO2.
• Enhanced Safety Standards aligned with international regulations and industry practices.
• Environmental Risk Assessments to ensure minimal ecological impact during transport.
• Digital Reporting Tools for streamlined data collection and transparency.
CDI’s Chairman of the Board (Capt Jan Antonsson) said
“I am very pleased with CDI’s development of this new inspection program for these vessel types, carrying cargoes that present unique risks and require its unique controls. I would like to thank CDI staff and its Technical Committee developing this program so effectively, evolving from the latest CDI-M questionnaire, updating its system and process, and training qualified CDI inspectors in its use. Its launch offers CDI participants an industry-leading program for management of maritime risk when using these vessel types in their energy transition journey.”
CDI’s General Manager (Capt Howard Snaith) said
“This CDI program represents a significant step forward in supporting the maritime industry’s transition to low-carbon operations. By establishing rigorous inspection standards for LCO2 carriers, CDI is helping to build trust, safety, and accountability in a rapidly evolving field.”
The LCO2 Ship Inspection Program will be launched on the 12th of November 2025 and will be available to CDI members and stakeholders involved in the operation and chartering of specialised vessels transporting Liquefied Carbon Dioxide.

New functionalities within CDI-Marine regarding comments to Observations
For the attention of all CDI Marine Ship Operators, Chemical Company Participants and Stakeholders
- Please cascade this information within your companies and with your own stakeholders
From the 1st August 2025 the following new functionalities will be available within CDI-Marine regarding comments to Observations
1. CDI-Marine: Upload documents direct to any Ship Inspection Report Observation
From the 1st August 2025, Ship Operators will be able to upload multiple documents with a paperclip button as part of the ship operator comments to any raised observation. Various file types will be supported such as .PDF, .JPG, DOCX. .XLSX etc.
This option will only be available to the ship operators in the 14-day exclusive window to add comments.
During this period the ship operator can add (and/or remove) documents to any raised observation. In other words, the ship operator can only add documents to questions with a No answer and inspector’s observations.
• After the 14-day period, it will not be allowed anymore to add or remove documents.
This option is available to the ship operators in the 14-day exclusive window to add comments.
• After the 14-day period, it will not be allowed to remove or make changes to these existing initial comments and initial uploaded documents, these comments and uploaded documents are locked for editing.
These initial comments will be labelled as “Initial comments”:
View and download documents
The Ship Operator and the Chemical Company Participants, who have purchased the report, will be able to view the corresponding documents uploaded to the observations via hyperlinks to the documents.
• In the downloaded PDF file of a SIR report the names of the documents will be listed next to the printed observations.
• In addition, Ship Operators and Chemical Company Participants, will be able to download all documents in one go for the SIR in a zip file.
• The names of the downloaded documents will include a reference to the corresponding report and question numbers.
2. NOTE: Phase out “Ship File” and “Ship Documents” options
The new ‘add documents to ship operator comments’ facility will replace the existing “Ship File” and “Ship Documents” options.
• The Ship File and Ship Documents options will remain temporarily available for the existing active reports, only in a read-only state.
• Users can still access these sections, but the ship operators will not be able to add and/or remove content anymore.
• After archive of a SIR report, the Ship File and Ship Documents option will be removed for that ship.
• After max. 1 year (time period a SIR remains active) these options will not exist anymore in the database for all ships.
3. Introduction of NEW “Subsequent Comments/Documents” Function
CDI has received constructive feedback in regards to the planned phasing out of the CDI Marine Ship File and Ship Document file. Having listened to this feedback and taking it into account, CDI has put in place the following NEW functions to enable
ship operators to be able to add “subsequent comments” to a Ship Inspection Report (SIR) after the report is released to active status, (This is in addition to the “Initial Comments” before the SIR is released).
• After the inspection report becomes active and as long as the report stays active, the ship operator will have the option to add subsequent comments and upload additional documents to any raised observation and to the general remarks in every section.
• The subsequent comments/documents will be date stamped in the report and this date will be displayed in the report. These comments will be labelled as “Subsequent comments”, followed by the date stamp.
• Subsequent comments and uploaded documents are locked for editing for the ship operator once uploaded to the database. The ship operator can add multiple subsequent comments and attachment during the validity of the report.
• The initial and subsequent comments and all document names will be displayed in the printed / PDF version of the inspection report:
• In addition, the initial and subsequent comments and all document names will be displayed in the Excel output of the inspection report:
• The ship operators and chemical company members will be able to download all attached documents in the View Inspection Report screen in one go in a Zip file:
• The names of the downloaded documents will include a reference to the corresponding report number and question number, including the initial document name:
• When subsequent comments or documents have been added to the inspection report, this will be visualised in the list of ships and reports with a Note symbol icon and the date of the last subsequent comments:
• In the Ships and Report page the option will be added to filter on inspection reports where ship operators have added subsequent comments since a specific date:
• In the Ship Details page the date of the last subsequent comments will be displayed:

The Stichting Chemical Distribution Institute (CDI) Board of Directors held their recent meeting in Rotterdam on the 12
th
of March 2025.
It was announced at this meeting that after 5 years holding the position as a Director of the Stichting Chemical Distribution Institute (CDI), Mr Alfred de Jong, LyondellBasell would stand down as a Director of CDI, due to an internal change of position within LyondellBasell.
Alfred was first elected and subsequently appointed as a Director of CDI on the 20
th
of October 2020.
The CDI Board of Directors and staff extends its collective thanks and great appreciation to Alfred, for his clear guidance and assistance in helping the continuing development, promotion and growth of CDI over the last 5 years and wish him every success in his new position within LyondellBasell.

13 December 2024
CDI Announces Launch of 8th Edition “Terminal Inspection Report” (TIR) - 6th Jan 25
CDI is pleased to announce the release and launch of the 8th edition TIR
- i.e. “Go Live” on the 6th January 2025
1. Transition from 7th to 8th Edition TIR
The transition from the 7th Edition TIR to the 8th Edition TIR will take place over midnight Central European Time (CET) on the 5th/6th January 2025
NOTE 1: Progresity will send an email to all CDI-T inspectors with the download, installation and user instructions for the new CDI-T Report Editor on Friday 3rd January 2025 (This email will contain the e-mail address assurance.support@progresity.com)
NOTE 2: When a CDI-T inspector completes a new report using the 8th edition TIR, the terminal operator will be sent an automatic email to access their new report
NOTE 3: Terminal operators with an active 7th edition TIR will continue to be able to access their report in the usual manner
NOTE 4: Chemical company participants will continue to be able to access any active 7th edition and/or 8th edition TIR in the usual manner
 Any 7th Edition TIR inspection which commences before the 6th January will be able to uploaded after the 6th January 2025 as a 7th Edition TIR
 From the 6th January 2025 all CDI-T inspections will only be undertaken using the 8th Edition TIR
2. New Functionality and changes in the 8th Edition TIR
The primary changes to the 8th edition TIR follows a review of current best practices and extensive feedback from stakeholders by the CDI-T Technical Committee.
However, there are also some new and updated enhancements available as part of the 8th Edition TIR
i. NEW Online (Voluntary) “Terminal Self Inspection Document” (T-SID) Editor
This new function will be available for all Terminal Operators to be able to complete all “Self Inspection” (S.I.) designated questions as well as “Terminal Particular Questions” (TPQ) designated questions.
Once completed, the “T-SID”, (containing the “SI” & “TPQ” questions), can be electronically sent to the selected CDI-Terminal inspector and imported into the Inspectors Report Editor Software by the CDI inspector
Each Terminal Operator will need to register a CDI-T user account (by completing the online registration form) to use the Online T-SID Editor and to complete the Self-Inspection Document.
For the Terminal Operator: The general working procedure with the On-line T-SID will be as follows:
• Terminal operator to be CDI-T inspected: Navigates to the “Online T-SID Editor” menu section on the www.cdit.nl website
• Registers the user account (if no user account registered yet)
• Logs on with the registered user account to the “Online T-SID Editor”
• Completes the Online T-SID prior to the CDI-T inspection
• Submits the completed T-SID to the selected CDI-Terminals inspector
• The CDI-Terminals database will send an automatic e-mail notification to confirm the T-SID submission to the inspector, and sends an automatic e-mail to notify the Terminal Operator.
For the CDI-Terminal Inspector:
• Inspector receives the e-mail notification about the receipt of the T-SID
• Inspector opens and evaluates the T-SID in CDI-Terminals Report editor
• Inspector samples 25% of the T-SID and if satisfied imports SID data in the CDI-Terminals Report Editor
• If the inspector has doubts, regarding SID veracity the inspector will revert to a full Terminal inspection
ii. UPDATED: Voluntary Sustainability Terminal Screening Updates and Functionality
Voluntary Sustainability Screening option was introduced in the 6th Edition TIR. However, this is now updated to reflect new “Together for Sustainability” (TfS) Questionnaire version 4.0 and 8th Edition TIR
• A chemical company member can request the specific terminal operator to (voluntarily) complete these additional sustainability questions via the “Sustainability Addendum” and import them into the CDI-T terminal company terminal report.
• Within the 8th edition TIR, existing CDI questions, that that relate to specific TfS questions are indicated with the designator “SUS” when viewed in he CDI-T database
• This enables the chemical company member to extract and review all sustainability related questions (both from the CDI-T Report and the Sustainability Addendum) for their (TfS) Sustainability screening requirements in the CDI-T database in an effective simple manner
iii. General 8th Edition TIR Updates
Revision work has been extensive and taken many months to review, revise and update the 8th edition. Updates include
• A full review to ensure it remains salient and up to date
• Extraction and inclusion of relevant elements from CDI’s “International Marine Packed Cargo Scheme “ (IMPCAS), in order to enhance the following areas
• Warehousing
• Tank Container Operations & Tank Cleaning Stations
• Contractual agreements
• Risk Management
• Due diligence
• Underwriting
• Insurance policy requirements

23 May 2024
29th May 2024: CDI web site updates and New Ship Inspection Request Database
For the Attention of all CDI Stakeholders: CDI web site updates and new Ship Inspection Request Database
CDI stakeholders are advised that CDI is launching its new web site, CDI home page and new Ship Inspection Request Database on the 29th May 2024
1. New CDI web site & Domain name, from the 29th May 2024:
From the 29th May 2024, the new CDI web site home page domain name will be www.cdim.org
i. The old domain name (www.cdi.org.uk) will be closed down from the 29th May, but will be linked to the new domain home page
- Consequently, any users searching the old domain name will be automatically directed to the new CDI home page domain name www.cdim.org
ii. Whilst a familiarity to the CDI web site is being retained to assist in navigating the CDI web site, a fresh new look has none the less been introduced.
2. New “Ship Inspection Request Database” from the 29th May 2024
From the 29th May 2024, all CDI Marine ship operators will have to log-in to the new Ship Inspection Request database in order to submit an inspection request to CDI
i. Existing Ship Operators, already registered with CDI, will be able to login to their existing CDI account using their existing CDI user name and password and then select a vessel from their own fleet, select vessel type and inspection type(s) in the usual manner
ii. New Ship Operators who are not registered with CDI, will be invited to contact the CDI Ship Inspection Department for the required Annex B agreement and registration form, in order to register their fleet with CDI and be issued with a user name and password
