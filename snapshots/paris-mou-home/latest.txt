Inspection Regime
The Paris MoU enforces safety at sea through Port State Control (PSC).
PSC involves the inspection of foreign ships in national ports. These inspections verify that the vessel’s condition, equipment, manning, and operation fully comply with international maritime regulations.
By targeting sub-standard shipping, our inspection regime ensures a safer, more secure, and environmentally sustainable maritime industry.
