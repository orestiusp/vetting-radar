What is Repsol Vetting?
We are a team of marine experts in charge of ensuring that the vessels involved in the Repsol Group's undertakings meet Repsol's criteria and process standards.
Our
Vetting
team is in charge of carrying out
assessments
in accordance with our vetting processes at both Repsol terminals and other national and international terminals.
Vessel requirements
Vessels owned by the Repsol Group.
Time charter vessels.
Voyage charter vessels.
Any ship operating at a Repsol Group terminal even if this operation is undertaken by an Operator not belonging to the Group.
Any unchartered vessel carrying cargo belonging to the Repsol group.
A range of vetting processes are available to adapt to the type of vessel being screened
Repsol Vetting Process
Vessel types
This process applies to
vessels
regulated by the
SIRE and EBIS
programs,
cargo ships, and tugs.
Vessel vetting assessment
The vetting assessment starts with a request from one of the Repsol business units, and it's regulated by the Repsol Vetting Process and Marine Safety Criteria document (which can be downloaded below).
Repsol Vetting does not pre-approve vessels, so they must be screened each time they are nominated by a Repsol Group business.
Vetting request
If you are interested in arranging an inspection for your vessel, please email us a formal request
at least five days beforehand
indicating the
port, date, local agents, and type of operations
.
Inspection areas
Our main inspection areas are located in
Europe and the Americas.

Repsol Vetting Process for F(P)SOs
Vessel types
This process is applicable to
Floating Storage and Offloading Units
(FSO) and
Floating Production Storage Offloading Units
(FPSO).
Vessel vetting assessment
The vetting assessment starts with a request from one of the Repsol business units, and it's regulated by the Repsol Vetting Marine Assurance Process & Safety Criteria for F(P)SOs document, which can be downloaded below.
Compliance of F(P)SOs with the requirements does not grant the Owner or Operator any right whatsoever to have the F(P)SO chartered or employed by Repsol.
Vetting request
Requested by one of Repsol's business units.
Vetting frequency
For the duration of the contract, the F(P)SOs are subject to a physical inspection by Repsol at
intervals not exceeding 12 months
.

Repsol Offshore Vetting Process
Vessel types
We apply this process to
vessels under the OCIMF-OVID program
.
Vessel vetting assessment
The assessment of the vessel will begin once requested by a Repsol company or business unit. The assessment will be carried out in accordance with the OVVP (Offshore Vessel Vetting Process), which can be downloaded below.
Vetting request
Requested by one of our Repsol companies or Business Unit.
Requirements
If you need a vessel to be assessed through the Vetting process, please make sure that the vessel is registered under the OCIMF‑OVID programme. An up‑to‑date OVPQ (
updated within the last 12 months
) must be available in accordance with OCIMF‑OVID requirements. You can complete and maintain the OVPQ via the OCIMF‑OVID website. Further guidance is available at the link below.
