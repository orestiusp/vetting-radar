(Concentrated) Inspection Campaign Results
To maintain high safety and environmental standards across the maritime industry, the Paris MoU – often jointly with
the Tokyo MoU – regularly launch
a Concentrated Inspection Campaign (CIC) or a Focused Inspection Campaign (FIC).
A Concentrated Inspection Campaign is a targeted, three-month inspection initiative that uses
standardized questionnaires to enforce compliance with specific international safety and environmental regulations.
Following these inspections, the
results are analysed and presented to the governing bodies of the MoUs for potential submission to the International Maritime Organization (IMO) to influence global maritime policy.
