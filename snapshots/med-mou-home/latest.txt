Membership of Croatia
First of January 2024
From the first of January 2024, the Republic of Croatia will be full member to the Med MoU. Membership of Croatia will add an important coast line to our Memorandum in the Adriatic Sea. On behalf of the Chairman and all Member States the Med MoU Secretariat would like to welcome Croatia as the eleventh member state to the Med MoU.
Membership of France
First of January 2026
The Mediterranean MoU on PSC is pleased to announce the formal acceptance of the French
Republic as its newest member. The decision was ratified during the 27th
Committee Session of the Med MoU, held from 21 to 23 October 2025 in
Kalkara, Malta. The accession was formally signed during the session, with
membership set to become effective on 1 January 2026.
