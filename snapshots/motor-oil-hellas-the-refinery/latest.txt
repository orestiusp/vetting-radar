About us
The Refinery
The refinery, with its ancillary plants and fuel distribution facilities, forms the largest privately owned industrial complex in Greece and is considered one of the most modern refineries in Europe
The Refinery is Located in Agioi Theodoroi, Corinth, about 70 km from the Centre of Athens.
The refinery, with its ancillary plants and fuel distribution facilities, forms the largest privately owned industrial complex in Greece and is considered one of the most modern refineries in Europe. The complexity of Motor Oil’s refinery stands at 12.61 according to Nelson’s Complexity Index.
It has the capacity to process various types of crude oil and manufactures a wide range of oil products. It supplies commercial oil companies in Greece, but the bulk of its production is sold abroad. Moreover, it is the only refinery that produces base oils in Greece. Refinery products meet the European Union specifications, as well as the most stringent international standards. The technical sophistication of the refinery allows Motor Oil to manufacture products with high added value, adjusting the final product mix to market needs, ensuring better distribution prices and achieving better refining margins than other composite refineries in the Mediterranean.
Characteristics of the Refinery
Processing capacity: 200,000+ barrels of crude oil per stream day (BSD).
The refinery produces all types of fuel and is one of the most advanced and complex in Europe, with Hydrocracker and Catalytic Cracking units and has a 12.61 rating on the Nelson Complexity Index.
It produces refined fuels (gasoline and automotive diesel) in accordance with EU specifications.
It has ISO 9001:2015 certification for Quality, ISO 14001:2015 for the Environment, OHSAS 18001:2007 certification for Health and Safety, ISO 17025:2005 certification for its Chemical Labs and CE Marking certification for its asphalt products, in accordance with Regulation (EU) No 305/2011 and the requirements of European standard ΕΝ 12591:2009. This is a combination of standards found in very few European refineries. Furthermore, Motor Oil applies the EI/JIG1530 standard in relation to the production, storage and distribution of jet fuel and has been awarded the JIG AWARD SPA, maintaining the highest possible rating. In the context of reducing greenhouse gas emissions and the sustainability criteria laid down by the EU, our company applies a control system for biofuels and the blending of fossil fuels with biofuels, and has been certified with the 2BSvs Certificate since October 2016. This certification meets the requirements of the biofuel mass balance. In March 2023, the company was certified with the ISO 22301:2019 (BCP) standard, which concerns the continuity of the company’s operation from operational disruptions when they arise. The refinery has also been certified with three new certificates, ISO 50001:2018 for energy management, ISO 18788:2015 for security, and ISO 27001:2013 for information security management.
It is the only refinery in Greece with a unit producing base oils and finished lubricants, approved by such international agencies as the American Petroleum Institute (API), the European Automobile Manufacturers Association (ACEA) and the United States Army and Navy.
Motor Oil is also officially certified with the ISCC EU standard, one of the leading international certifications, this way reinforces the company’s commitment for sustainable development and environmental responsibility, ensuring Sustainable Resource Management, CO₂ Emission Reduction and Supply Chain Traceability. As part of our commitment to a sustainable future, Motor Oil publishes its annual environmental statement in accordance with the European Regulations (EC) 1221/2009, (EU) 1505/2017 and (EU) 2018/2026, for the Environmental Management and Audit System (EMAS).
It is the only refinery in Greece that has a production unit for base and final lubricants approved by international organisations such as the American Petroleum Institute (API), the European Automobile Manufacturers’ Association (ACEA) and the US Army and Navy.
It has a power plant consisting of five gas turbines with a total capacity of 85MW. In 2024, an additional high-efficiency Cogeneration Unit with a capacity of 57 MW was added.
It possesses a power and steam cogeneration unit, which now has a capacity of 85 MW following the recent addition of a fifth gas turbine.
It uses natural gas as a fuel and as a raw material for the production of hydrogen.
It has a storage capacity of 2,900,000 m3 – 1,000,000 cubic meters for crude oil and 1,900,000 cubic meters for intermediate and final products.
It has modern tanker berthing facilities with a capacity of up to 450,000 DWT, which can serve more than 3,000 vessels per year, as well as an offshore anchorage for crude unloading that can accommodate ships with a capacity of up to 300,000 DWT.
It has a modern tanker loading terminal, which can serve 220 tankers per day.
Refinery Units
Atmospheric Distillation Units:
Crude oil is the basic raw material in the entire production which after undergoing the first treatment in the Atmospheric Distillation Units (with a total capacity of 200,000+ barrels per day) is separated into LPG, naphtha, kerosene, diesel and fuel oil. The LPG, after the removal of sulfur compounds, is led to the spherical tanks for sale. The naphtha, kerosene, diesel and fuel oil undergo further processing in the individual complexes, to meet specific specifications.
Gasoline Production:
Naphtha, after undergoing desulfurisation by catalytic hydrogenation, is separated into two streams: heavy and light naphtha. A small amount of naphtha originating from the thermal cracking unit is processed in the same unit. The products produced are high-quality gasolines.
New Naphtha Upgrading Complex:
The New Naphtha Upgrading Complex began operation in 2022, increasing the refinery’s production of high-value products such as high-octane gasoline, kerosene and hydrogen. The capacity for gasoline production from naphtha was increased.
Hydrocracker Complex:
This particular unit is one of the company’s largest investments, amounting to €350 mn and was completed in 2005. The operation of the hydrocracker enabled the production of new clean fuels with low sulfur content in accordance with the European Union specifications of 2009 (Auto Oil II). The diesel production unit of the complex produces a product that exceeds EU specifications. In addition, the Hydrocracking Unit contributed decisively to the improvement of the environmental conditions of the refinery, since emissions from the Catalytic Cracking (FCC) complex were significantly reduced.
FCC Complex:
This complex uses as raw material the desulfurised VGO from the hydrocracking unit and fuel oil coming from the atmospheric distillation unit, or imported fuel oil, and produces LPG, high octane gasoline, diesel and fuel oil. Some of the LPG goes to other units and is converted into high quality gasoline components.
Lubes Production:
This is used as a raw material fuel oil from the atmospheric distillation unit or imported fuel oil and is distilled under vacuum to produce fractions for the production of lubricants. This is followed by processes that improve their properties, such as viscosity index, pour point and cloud point for the production of basic lubricants. In addition, asphalt is produced from the residue of the vacuum distillation which is either sold, or together with the residue from the corresponding vacuum distillation unit for the production of fuels, goes to the thermal cracking unit for the upgrading and production of fuel oil.
