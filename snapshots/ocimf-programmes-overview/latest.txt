Programmes
To ensure the highest standards are applied across the industry, OCIMF has developed inspection and self-assessment programmes to promote best practices and regulatory compliance in the marine industry.
With specific programmes developed for different segments of the marine industry, OCIMF’s programmes provide reliable, standardised and stringently managed risk-assessment tools to charterers, ship vessel operators, terminal operators and government bodies concerned with ship safety, security and the environment.
OCIMF’s programmes include the Ship Inspection Report Programme (SIRE), Barge Inspection Report Programme (BIRE), the Management Self Assessment (MSA) for tankers, barges and offshore vessels, the Offshore Vessel Inspection Database (OVID) for offshore vessels and mobile offshore drilling units and the Marine Terminal Information System (MTIS) for oil terminal operators and managers.
SIRE 2.0
A digitalised inspection programme that future proofs the tanker inspection process in line with evolving risks, technology and expertise. It has transformed how inspections are conducted and supports industry efforts to continuously enhance safety.

Barge Inspection Report Programme
BIRE is a barge and inland coastal vessel inspection regime aligned to OCIMF’s Ship Inspection Report Programme (SIRE) providing the marine industry with a comprehensive and standardised vessel risk assessment tool and vessel inspection report database.
Offshore Vessel Inspection Database
OVID is an inspection regime providing a comprehensive and standardised risk assessment tool and vessel inspection database, which are central to the selection and assurance of offshore vessels, enhancing safe and environmentally responsible operations in the industry.
